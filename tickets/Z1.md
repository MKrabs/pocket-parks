## Goal
A 16×16 isometric ground grid you can look around, shown by the main scene.

- `scripts/iso_grid.gd` (`class_name IsoGrid`, pure logic, no nodes): tile size 64×32 (2:1),
  `SIZE := Vector2i(16, 16)`, `to_screen(cell: Vector2i) -> Vector2` (tile centre),
  `to_cell(screen: Vector2) -> Vector2i`, `in_bounds(cell) -> bool`.
- `scripts/grid_view.gd`: a Node2D that draws every tile as a slightly rounded iso diamond
  (soft corners, not sharp), checkered `Palette.GRASS` / `Palette.GRASS_DARK`. A small
  "ground slab" edge under the board (two darker side faces) so it reads as a 3D island.
  The tile under the mouse gets a `Palette.SKY` highlight. Emits `cell_hovered(cell)` and `cell_clicked(cell, button)`
  (Z3 will use them; it's just a signal, don't build placement).
- Camera2D: starts centred on the grid with the whole board visible at 1280×720. Pan with
  right- or middle-mouse drag and WASD/arrows, zoom with the wheel (clamped ~0.5–2.5).
- `main.gd` creates the grid view and the camera.

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_iso_grid.gd`: `to_cell(to_screen(c)) == c` for every cell, plus corners/edges
  and an out-of-bounds case.
- `./gd check` ok; `./gd shot shots/Z1.png` shows the whole island centred on the cream background.
