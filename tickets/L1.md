## Goal
`docs/design/game.md` → Park level.
- XP from guests served (and a bonus for happy departures). Levels 1–10 with rising thresholds (one const table).
- **Unlocks** (one table, shown in a small "Level up!" card with `Motion`): max building level (Lv 2 at park level 2,
  Lv 3 at park level 4), number of builder sheds (1 at Lv 1, +1 at 3, 5, 7), second builder specialty at park level 6.
  Leave room in the table for plots and themes later.
- The HUD level badge (X3) fills its ring and pops on level up. Shed placement and upgrade buttons respect the caps,
  with the reason shown when blocked.
