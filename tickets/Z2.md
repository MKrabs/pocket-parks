## Goal
The visual language for buildings, plus 5 sample buildings as data. Runs in parallel with Z1:
**do not touch `main.gd` or `main.tscn`**. Use the same iso tile size as Z1 (64×32, 2:1) and
take a cell-centre position as input, so Z3 can put these on the grid.

- `scripts/squircle.gd` (`class_name Squircle`, static, pure): `points(center, half_size, n := 4.0, steps := 32) -> PackedVector2Array`
  (superellipse), and an iso variant that projects a squircle footprint onto the 2:1 ground plane.
- Extruded squircle block: a footprint polygon raised by `height` px: top face in the base colour,
  the visible side faces in `Palette.shade(c, 0.12)` / `Palette.shade(c, 0.22)`. It has to read as a
  soft rounded block, not a box.
- `scripts/buildings.gd` (`class_name Buildings`): a catalog dictionary keyed by id. Each entry has
  `name`, `footprint: Vector2i` (tiles), `cost: int`, and whatever drawing parameters you need. Five samples:
  `path` (1×1, flat SAND squircle tile), `tree` (1×1, WOOD trunk + stacked MOSS squircle canopy),
  `snack_stand` (1×1, WOOD block + CLAY roof), `pond` (2×2, STONE rim + WATER inset),
  `enclosure` (3×3, low WOOD fence ring around a GRASS_DARK floor, with one STONE rock and one tree inside).
  Costs: your guess, roughly 5 / 20 / 60 / 80 / 150.
- `scripts/building_view.gd`: a Node2D that draws one catalog building, anchored at its origin cell's
  centre, covering `footprint` tiles. Colours only from Palette.
- `tests/show_buildings.gd`: a SceneTree script that lays out all 5 on a plain cream background, for
  `./gd shot shots/Z2.png 1 res://tests/show_buildings.gd`.

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_squircle.gd`: points are symmetric, all within half_size, n=2 gives an ellipse-ish shape,
  the catalog has 5 entries with positive cost and footprint.
