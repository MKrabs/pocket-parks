## Goal
Turn the zoo catalog into the first **theme**, and add a tiny second one to prove themes swap.
Read `docs/design/game.md` → Pillars and Building roles first.

- Every catalog entry gets `role`: one of `entrance`, `path`, `attraction`, `shop`, `decor`.
  Attractions and decor get `appeal: int`, shops get `price: int`, attractions get `capacity: int`.
  Your numbers. Keep them simple and name them in the handoff.
- `scripts/themes/zoo.gd`: today's 5 buildings plus a new **Zoo Gate** (entrance, 1×1, an arch made of
  two WOOD posts and a CLAY top).
- `scripts/themes/festival.gd`: Festival Arch (entrance), Boardwalk (path, WOOD), Stage (attraction 3×2),
  Big Grill (attraction 2×2), Food Truck (shop), Lantern Tree (decor, BUTTER lanterns). Same palette and squircle language.
- `Buildings` becomes the lookup over the active theme: `Buildings.use(theme_id)`, `Buildings.get_def(id)`,
  `Buildings.ids()`, `Buildings.ids_with_role(role)`. Building ids are unique across themes (e.g. `fest_stage`),
  so a stolen blueprint from another theme can still be looked up (`Buildings.get_def` searches all themes).
- `T` key toggles the active theme in the running game: the build bar shows that theme's buildings,
  and the placed ones stay.
- Seeded starting park includes the gate, with the path connected to it.
