## Goal
The host side of "Sending people" (`docs/design/game.md` → Crime reports, Building pins). It happens in **your own park**.

- **Bot senders:** a couple of named bot "friends" send mixed groups into your park now and then (mostly
  guests, sometimes 1–2 actors). Rate in one const.
- **Crime reports:** toasts stacked top-right (rounded, palette, max 4, fade after ~6 s) for every `events`
  entry: icon + "Snack Stand was robbed: −$24" / "Pond was wrecked" / "Litter around Enclosure" /
  "Someone studied the Stage". "Seen by a guard" when witnessed. Clicking a toast pans the camera to the building.
- **Building pins:** above any building with a `building_status`, a small map-pin bubble (squircle head with a
  pointed tail, drawn with polygons) with a simple drawn icon per status: wrecked = broken/cracked glyph in CORAL
  ("needs rebuilding"), littered = specks/broom in SAND ("needs cleaning"), robbed = coin in BUTTER (fades).
  Hovering shows the text. Clicking a wrecked pin repairs it (`repair`, shows the cost). Pins float a little, bob
  gently and keep a fixed screen size when zooming.
- **Accuse:** an "Accuse" tool in the build bar (next to Eraser). With it, clicking a person accuses them (`accuse`),
  and a toast shows the outcome: "Caught a thief: +$90 fine" or "Wrongful arrest: you paid $40". Wanted actors
  show the CORAL "!" to the host too.
- Fines and wrongful-arrest payments change your money (bots settle immediately).
