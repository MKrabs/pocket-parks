## Goal
The sender side of "Sending people" (`docs/design/game.md`). Bot parks stand in for friends.

- HUD **Visit** button → a small rounded picker of 3 nearby parks (bot parks via `BotPark.generate`; mix themes;
  each has 0–3 guards and a made-up friendly name). Picking one swaps the view to that park, running live. **Your
  own park's sim keeps running** in the background at 1×.
- While visiting: a **Send** panel (rounded, palette) with one button per kind: Guest $3, Hooligan $40,
  Litterbug $20, Thief $50, Spy $60. Each press sends one person, paid now (your guess on the prices, keep
  them in one const).
- Your own people are visible *to you* with a subtle marker (a small dot above the bean, colour by kind).
  Everyone else looks like a guest. Wanted actors get a tiny CORAL "!".
- A ledger strip: your people inside (guests / hidden / wanted), earned so far (guest share + delivered
  loot), fines paid. **Back home** returns you; people you sent keep going in that park until they leave
  (keep simulating visited parks you've left, up to 3).
- Stolen blueprints are added to your build bar (as the old raid flow did).
