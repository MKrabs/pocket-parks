## Goal
Owner: "When I select trees they're always active. I need a way to deselect and have a blank cursor." Plus an
info UI for buildings. Spec: `docs/design/game.md` → Building (Placing) and Screen layout.

- **Select is the default mode** (nothing preselected at start). Hover highlights the building or person under the
  mouse (a soft outline or lift, not the tile highlight) and shows a small tooltip: "Snack Stand · Lv 2".
- Clicking a build-bar item turns on place mode with the ghost. **Esc, right-click, or clicking the same item again**
  returns to Select. Path and tree stay active after placing (to paint); everything else returns to Select after one
  placement unless Shift is held.
- **Building card:** a rounded side card that slides in on the right (use `Motion`) when you click a building in Select.
  Name + level, role line (e.g. "Shop · sells at $8"), today's guests and income for it, its current status in words
  (wrecked: "Wrecked. Reopens in 32 s"; littered; robbed; spied), and action buttons: **Upgrade** (price; disabled with
  the reason at max level or when too poor), **Repair now** (only when wrecked), **Demolish** (refund shown). Esc or a
  click on empty ground closes it. It updates live.
- **Person card:** clicking a guest or staff shows mood, what they've spent, and for guests an **Accuse** button with
  the warning "If they're innocent you pay $40". Your own staff: kind only, for now.
- **Remove from the build bar:** Eraser, Upgrade, Accuse (their actions now live in the cards). Right-click no longer erases.
- **Remove the level dots** from `building_view.gd` (keep the flags and canopy).
- Clicking a pin opens that building's card.

## Acceptance

  stays active after placing, a snack stand doesn't; Shift keeps it; selecting a building opens the card with the right
