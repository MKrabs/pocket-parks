## Goal
Owner plays on a laptop trackpad (GNOME, Wayland). A two-finger swipe should **pan the camera slowly and
smoothly**, in the swipe's direction. Mouse users keep wheel = zoom. Only `scripts/iso_camera.gd` (plus a test).

- `InputEventPanGesture` → pan by `delta`, scaled (macOS and any backend that sends it).
- Linux reports two-finger scrolling as `InputEventMouseButton` wheel events (UP/DOWN/LEFT/RIGHT) with a
  fractional `factor` (a burst of small values), while a mouse notch has `factor` ≈ 1. Treat a wheel event with
  `0 < factor < 0.9`, and any WHEEL_LEFT/RIGHT, as a **pan** of `factor * PAN_STEP` px (screen space, divided by
  zoom). A factor ≈ 1 (or 0) stays zoom.
- `Ctrl`+wheel always zooms. `InputEventMagnifyGesture` (pinch) zooms around its factor.
- Keep the pan logic in a small static function (event → pan offset or zoom factor), so it can be tested without a window.
- Tunables at the top (`PAN_STEP`, gesture scale), commented, because the owner will tune the feel.

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_camera_input.gd`: a fractional vertical wheel pans and doesn't zoom; a factor-1 wheel zooms; horizontal
  wheel pans; Ctrl+fractional wheel zooms; a pan gesture pans; panning is smaller when zoomed in.
- `./gd check` ok. Handoff: "needs an owner test on the trackpad" plus the tunables to adjust.
