## Goal
**R** rotates the placement ghost by 90° (Shift+R the other way). A rotation button also shows in the X1 card for
not-yet-built ghosts if that's easy (skip it otherwise).
- `ParkMap` stores a rotation (0–3) per building. Footprints rotate (3×2 becomes 2×3). `can_place` and occupancy use
  the rotated footprint.
- `BuildingView` draws parts rotated around the footprint centre. Asymmetric buildings must visibly turn: the gate's
  arch direction, the stage's back wall, the food truck's hatch.
- The sim's doors and path adjacency work for every rotation.
## Acceptance
`tests/test_rotation.gd`: the rotated footprint occupies the right cells; can_place respects it; a rotated stage next to a
path gets guests. Shot: the gate and the stage in all 4 rotations (show script). `./gd check` ok.
