## Goal
`docs/design/game.md` → Building: construction and upgrades take time and need a builder.
- New building in every theme: **Builder Shed** (1×1, role `staff`, a small wooden shed with a hard-hat sign). The
  start park has one shed with one builder, free. Each shed houses exactly one builder (named, e.g. "Bo").
- Placing or upgrading creates a **job** (pure logic in `ParkSim` or a small `Construction` class): the money is paid up
  front, the site is closed, and the job waits for a free builder, who walks from their shed to the site and works. Build
  times: path instant, tree 5 s, shop 20 s, pond 40 s, enclosure 60 s, others by size; upgrades ×1.5 (Lv 2) / ×2.5 (Lv 3).
  One const table.
- The site shows a **scaffold** (wood frame plus a SAND tarp, squircle style) and a progress ring over it. It finishes
  with a `Motion` pop. Upgrades show the scaffold around the existing building.
- Builders are drawn as beans with a BUTTER hard hat. The building card (X1) shows construction progress and the
  builder, and "Waiting for a builder" when all are busy. The Staff tab (or the shed's card) shows each builder.
- Demolishing a site under construction refunds everything.
