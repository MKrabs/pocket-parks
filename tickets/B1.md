## Goal
Raid targets need parks. `scripts/bot_park.gd` (`class_name BotPark`, pure): `generate(theme_id, seed, size := "small") -> ParkMap`
(or `ZooMap` if S1 hasn't renamed it yet on your branch; keep the call sites trivial to rename).

- Entrance on an edge, a connected path network (a spine plus a couple of branches), attractions and
  shops placed next to paths, decor sprinkled around, using only buildings of that theme. Valid placement
