## Goal
Buildings can be upgraded from level 1 to 3 (`docs/design/game.md` → Roadmap M3).

- `ParkMap` stores a level per placed building (default 1). `upgrade(origin) -> bool`: level 2 costs 1× the
  building's cost, level 3 costs 2×; refused at max level or when too poor. Removing a building refunds half of
  everything paid, upgrades included.
- Effect (one helper, so the sim and the UI agree): per level above 1, attractions get +50% appeal and capacity,
  shops +50% price, decor +50% appeal. Entrance and path don't upgrade. `ParkSim` uses the leveled values.
- Visible on the model: each level adds a small extra (an extra roof tier, a BUTTER trim or flag, a bigger canopy)
  in the same squircle style, plus 1–3 tiny level dots at the building's front. Keep it subtle and readable at normal zoom.
- UI: an **Upgrade** tool in the build bar (next to Eraser). Clicking a building with it upgrades it; the ghost/hover
  shows the price, or CORAL when that's not possible.
