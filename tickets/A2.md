## Goal
Use the A1 motion kit (`Motion`, puffs) so the player *notices* what happens. Constants stay in `motion.gd`.

**Crimes (priority):** when a crime event arrives, the building gives a short `shake`. Wrecked: a few small
debris puffs, and the building's existing grey tilt eases in instead of snapping. Robbed: a small BUTTER coin
pops out of the shop and flies away. Littered: a tiny puff. Spied: a quick SKY glint. New pins `pop` in, and pins and
crime reports `vanish` when they go.

**Money:** a floating "+$5" (small, rounded, BUTTER) rises and fades over the shop or gate where a guest pays. Batch
it if needed so it never turns into noise (e.g. at most one per building per second, summed). The HUD money chip counts
up or down to the new value with a small bounce. Fines in: a coin flies to the chip. Wrongful arrest: a coin flies away
from it, CORAL.
If `ParkSim` needs a lightweight record of payments or arrests for this (e.g. `cash_events`, `arrests` like
`events`), add it as pure data with a test.

**Arrests:** the guard does a little hop. The caught actor vanishes in a puff. The same goes for an Accuse click.

**Building:** placing does a `squish` plus a dust puff at the base. Removing shrinks into a puff. Upgrading bounces,
and the new flag or extra pops in.
