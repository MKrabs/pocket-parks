## Goal
Raids per `docs/design/game.md` → Raids, as pure logic on top of `ParkSim`.

- `ParkSim.start_raid(squad: Array[String])`: raiders enter at the entrance over the first few seconds,
  the raid ends at 60 s or when no raiders are left. Types and behaviour exactly as the doc's table:
  hooligan (wreck → `closed[origin] = true`, guests avoid it and get unhappy), litterbug, thief (steal,
  must escape via the entrance), spy (watch the highest-appeal building, must escape → blueprint id).
- Guards catch a raider within 1 tile (the raider is removed, the guard pauses 2 s). Raiders are a little
  faster than guests and slower than guards.
- `raid_result()`: `{stars: Array[bool] (3, per the doc), stolen_money: int, blueprints: Array[String],
  wrecked: int, end_happiness: float}`. Stolen money leaves the target's money.
- `end_raid()` reverts temporary damage (closed buildings reopen, raid litter is removed).

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_raid.gd`, deterministic with a seed:
  - a hooligan wrecks something when there are no guards;
  - with 3 guards around the entrance a lone thief is usually caught (most of 20 seeds);
  - a successful spy yields a blueprint id that `Buildings.get_def` resolves;
  - `end_raid` reopens everything;
  - stars match the result.
- `./gd check` ok.
