## Goal
Replace the raid mode with the owner's mechanic. Read `docs/design/game.md` → Pillar 3 and "Sending people"
first; it is the spec. Pure logic in `ParkSim`, plus deleting the old raid mode so nothing references it.

**Remove:** `start_raid/raid_result/end_raid`, the 60 s timer and the stars in `park_sim.gd`; `scripts/raid.gd`,
`scripts/raid_panel.gd`, the Raid button in `hud.gd`, the raid wiring in `main.gd`,
`tests/test_raid*.gd`, `tests/show_raid*.gd`. Keep `BotPark` (parks to visit) and the actor behaviours that
still fit (wreck, litter, steal, spy, guard chase).

**Add to ParkSim:**
- `send(kind, sender: String)`: kind = `guest | hooligan | litterbug | thief | spy`; the person queues at the gate
  and enters like a guest. Guests from a sender pay that sender 20% of what they spend.
- Actor states: `hidden` (behaves exactly like a guest: same walking, queueing and visits) → `striking`
  (doing the crime for a few seconds, exposed) → `hidden` again → after each crime it decides to leave or stay.
  Stay chance by crimes done: 0.8, 0.55, 0.3, 0.1, then 0. Loot is carried and paid to the sender only when it
  leaves through the gate.
- Crimes: hooligan wrecks an attraction or shop (closed, auto-reopens after 45 s; `repair(origin)` reopens now
  for 25% of the building's cost). Litterbug drops several litter around a building. Thief takes 15% of the
  park's money (cap 60). Spy copies the building's blueprint id.
- Every crime appends to `events: Array` `{tick, kind: "wrecked"|"littered"|"robbed"|"spied", origin: Vector2i,
  amount, witnessed: bool}`. It's reported to the host; it never says who did it.
- Guards: an actor striking within a guard's sight (3 tiles) is `witnessed`, marked `wanted`, and chased. Guards
  catch only `wanted` actors (within 1 tile). Never anyone else.
- Arrest → fine `BASE_FINE[kind] * crimes` (your bases, about 30–60) moves from the sender's ledger to the host, plus the
  carried loot back to the host.
- `accuse(person_id) -> Dictionary`: the host's manual arrest. An actor with crimes > 0: fine as above. An honest
  guest or an actor with 0 crimes: **wrongful arrest**: the host pays the sender `WRONGFUL_FINE` (about 40), and the person leaves.
- Money that flows to or from senders goes into `ledger: Dictionary` sender → int (positive = owed to the sender),
  so the UI and later the network can settle it.
- `building_status(origin) -> Array[String]`, from `wrecked`, `littered` (litter within 1 tile of the footprint),
  `robbed` (in the last 20 s): for the pins.

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_actors.gd`, seeded:
  - an actor looks hidden until its first strike;
  - stay/leave follows the chance table (statistically, over seeds);
  - loot reaches the ledger only on leaving;
  - a guard catches only witnessed actors and never an honest guest (over many seeds);
  - `accuse` on a 0-crime actor and on a guest is a wrongful arrest (the host pays);
  - `accuse` after 2 crimes fines base×2 plus loot back;
  - a wrecked building reopens after 45 s, and `repair` reopens it now and costs money;
  - honest guests pay their sender 20%;
  - events never contain the actor's id.
- No reference to raids left (`git grep -i raid` only in docs). `./gd check` ok. The game still runs (`./gd shot`).
