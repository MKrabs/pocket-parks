## Goal
The day simulation from `docs/design/game.md` → "A park day", as **pure logic, no nodes**, so it
can be tested and later reused for raids.

- `scripts/park_sim.gd` (`class_name ParkSim`): built from a `ZooMap` (rename it to `ParkMap` while
  you're here; update callers) plus a seed. `step()` advances one tick (10 ticks/s). It's deterministic for a given seed.
- Guests: spawn at the entrance, at a rate that grows with total appeal. They walk path tiles only (use
  `AStarGrid2D`), a building is reachable from a path tile next to its footprint. They pick a target
  weighted by appeal, queue if it's at capacity, pay the shop price or entry fee, sometimes drop litter
  on a path tile after a shop, and leave via the entrance when satisfied or unhappy. Happiness 0–100 as in the doc.
- Staff: `hire("janitor"|"guard")` (costs money up front, wage taken at day end). Janitors walk to the
  nearest litter and clean it. Guards patrol paths. Guards *catching* raiders comes in R1; leave that out.
- Read-only state for views: guests (cell, sub-tile position for smooth drawing, mood), litter cells,
  staff, money, day time, and `closed: Dictionary` (building origin → true; empty for now, R1 uses it).
- `day_report()` at 120 s: income, guests served, avg happiness.

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_park_sim.gd`: same seed gives the same report. No path to the entrance means no guests.
  More appeal means more guests. A janitor reduces litter vs. none. Guests never stand on non-path
  tiles (except the entrance).
- `./gd check` ok. Performance: a full 120 s day with ~60 guests simulates in under 1 s headless (print the time).
