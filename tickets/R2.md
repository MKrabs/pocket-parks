## Goal
The playable raid loop, from the player's own park.

- A **Raid** button in the HUD opens a rounded panel: 3 target cards (bot parks from `BotPark.generate`
  with random seeds; mix themes, at least one festival), each showing theme, size and a guard count
  (small: 0–1, large: 2–3, hired in the target's sim).
- Squad picker: +/− per raider type with its cost, total vs. your money. **Go** deducts the cost.
- The raid view swaps the world to the target park (your park's sim pauses), running the target's day
  with your squad: 60 s, a "Raid 0:42" timer, raiders visibly different (darker bean plus a tiny
  role mark: hooligan CORAL, litterbug SAND, thief INK, spy SKY). Wrecked buildings look grey/tilted.
- Finish card: 3 stars with their labels, loot. **Back home** returns to your park: money added, stolen
  blueprints appear in your build bar (works across themes: a festival Stage can stand in your zoo).
