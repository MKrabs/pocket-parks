## Goal
Make the player's park *live*: `main.gd` runs a `ParkSim` on the player's map and you watch the day.

- Guests: tiny pastel squircle "beans" (about 6–8 px, a few palette colours, a slight bob while walking),
  drawn with correct iso depth among buildings. Mood shows as a small tint or face dot. Litter: tiny specks
  on path tiles. Staff: same bean shape, but bigger and with a cap colour (janitor SAND, guard SKY).
- HUD (rounded, palette, same style as the build bar): day clock as a thin progress bar, money, guest count,
  average happiness, speed buttons (pause / 1× / 3×), "Hire janitor" and "Hire guard" buttons with prices.
- Building while the day runs is allowed. The sim must pick up map changes (rebuild the path graph).
- End of day: a small rounded summary card (income, guests, happiness, wages) and a "Next day" button.
