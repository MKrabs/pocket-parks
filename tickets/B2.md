## Goal
`docs/design/game.md` → Building (Specialties). **No XP or training: every specialty is available.**
- In a builder's card: choose **one** specialty from Landscaper, Water, Aviary, Heavy, Carpenter. A **second** slot
  appears only from park level 6 (L1). Show only the choices the builder can make or already has. Never show locked ones.
- Each building gets an optional `specialty` plus a mode: `only` (specialist-only) or `faster` (anyone at 1×, the
  specialist at 2×). Tag the existing zoo buildings sensibly (tree → Landscaper faster, pond → Water faster,
  snack stand → Carpenter faster, ...).
- New specialist-only zoo buildings, in the same squircle style: **Water Tank Enclosure** (3×3, glassy WATER tank with a
  STONE rim, attraction; Water), **Aviary** (3×3, netted dome of thin WOOD arcs, attraction; Aviary), **Fountain**
  (1×1 decor with a high appeal; Water), **Reinforced Enclosure** (3×3, STONE walls; Heavy). The build bar marks them with
  the specialty's glyph. A job for them waits for a matching builder and says so in the card.
- The job assigner picks the fastest free builder for each job.
- Left over from B1: gate **builder shed placement** with `ParkLevel.cap("sheds", map.park_level())`. The Staff tab and the ghost show "More sheds at park level N" when blocked. Add it to the test.
