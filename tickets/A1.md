## Goal
A tiny motion language for the whole game, and the owner's cloud transition for visiting. Everything uses `Tween`
on drawn shapes, with no assets. **All durations, scales and easings go in one const block at the top of
`scripts/motion.gd`**, because the owner tunes timing by feel.

**Kit**
- `scripts/puff.gd`: the game's signature cloud puff, a Node2D drawing an overlapping cluster of squircles in a
  soft cloud colour with a slightly darker underside. Size and seed are parameters. Add `CLOUD` (a warm off-white) and, if
  needed, a cloud shadow tone to `palette.gd`; no Color literals elsewhere.
- `scripts/motion.gd` (`class_name Motion`, static): `pop(node)` (appear: scale 0 → overshoot → 1, about 0.25 s),
  `vanish(node, free := true)` (shrink and fade, about 0.15 s), `squish(node)` (squash and stretch, about 0.25 s),
  `shake(node, strength)`, `fly_to(node, to_pos, then: Callable)`, `puff_at(parent, pos, size)` (a puff that pops and fades).
  Each returns its Tween.

**Cloud transition** (`scripts/cloud_transition.gd`, a CanvasLayer above everything, about 1.2 s in total):
1. The camera zooms out 0.92 → 0.75 in about 0.3 s (ease in).
2. Two or three layers of large puffs roll in from the sides and cover the screen in about 0.35 s. The back layer
   is slower, for parallax. Leaving home they travel left to right, coming back home right to left.
3. A hold of about 0.2 s with the **destination's name** in big rounded text on the clouds ("Clover Fields", or "Home"
   when going back). The park swap happens here, via a callback.
4. The clouds part outward while the camera zooms back in 0.75 → 0.92 with a slight overshoot.
- A click or a key press skips to the end. Input to the world is blocked while it runs.
- Wire it in: the Visit picker and Back home use new `travel_to(offer)` / `travel_home()` in `main.gd`, which play the
  transition and call the existing `visit()` / `go_home()` at the hold. **Keep `visit()` / `go_home()` instant**, because
  tests call them directly.

## Acceptance
- `tests/test_motion.gd`: the transition calls the swap exactly once, at the midpoint; a skip still swaps exactly once
  and ends with the camera at its normal zoom; `travel_to` followed by `travel_home` leaves `main.visiting == false`. Drive time
  with process frames or by stepping the tweens. No real waiting beyond a few seconds.
- Shots, using a show script that triggers the transition: `shots/A1-in.png` (clouds closing), `shots/A1-hold.png`
  (full cover, park name readable), `shots/A1-out.png` (clouds parting over the destination). Plus one with a few
  standalone puffs at different sizes. Look at them: the clouds must feel soft and rounded, not like blobs of circles.
- `./gd check` ok. Handoff: the tuning constants and what only the owner can judge (timing, feel).
